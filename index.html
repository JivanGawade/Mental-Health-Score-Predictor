Discover the Patterns Behind Student Well-Being

MindMetric AI uses machine learning to estimate a mental health score from lifestyle, academic and social media usage patterns. It is built to help students and researchers explore how everyday habits relate to well-being, not to diagnose anyone.

How it works

Three steps from your habits to a model-generated estimate. Nothing is stored along the way.

  1. 1

    Enter Your Information

    Provide lifestyle, academic and social media usage details in a short, guided form.

  2. 2

    Machine Learning Analysis

    Your validated answers are sent to the FastAPI service, where the trained model processes them.

  3. 3

    View Your Prediction

    See the numerical score the backend returns, exactly as the model produced it.

Privacy and responsible use

  • This tool is intended for educational and research purposes.
  • Predictions are estimates, not diagnoses or medical advice.
  • Lifestyle and social media patterns alone cannot establish a person's mental health condition.
  • If you have concerns about your mental health, please reach out to a qualified professional.
  • Your answers are sent only to the prediction service to compute the score. This page does not save them in local storage, cookies or browser history, and does not send them to third-party services.
